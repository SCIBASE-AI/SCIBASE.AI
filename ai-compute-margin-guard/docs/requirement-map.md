# Requirement Map

| Issue #20 area | Covered by this module |
| --- | --- |
| Tiered subscription billing | Plan metadata supplies included compute credit, institutional type, and margin policy floor. |
| Usage-based AI compute billing | Jobs allocate token, GPU, storage, reproducibility-run, and overhead costs to accounts. |
| Cloud resource pricing | Provider SKUs model per-token, per-GPU-hour, storage, and run-level cloud costs. |
| Transparent quotas and meters | Account summaries show metered revenue, quota burn, included credit pressure, and unpaid top-ups. |
| Top-ups | Paid and unpaid top-ups are included in revenue recognition and hold decisions. |
| Institutional invoicing | Unknown SKUs, grant budget warnings, and gross-margin holds prevent unsafe invoice close. |
| Data licensing / analytics support | Pricing-floor notes and reviewer packet are safe to expose as aggregate finance evidence, not private content. |
| Long-term scalability and sustainability | Gross-margin floors, cheaper routing, and pricing floor notes protect high-margin AI compute operations. |

## Distinct slice

Existing issue #20 submissions cover subscription ledgers, entitlement engines, procurement controls, revenue recognition, tax treatment, licensing gates, and usage reconciliation. This module is narrower: it protects AI compute profitability by tying cloud cost allocation to gross-margin floors, route recommendations, top-up pricing, grant-budget risk, and invoice holds.
