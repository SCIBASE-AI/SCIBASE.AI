# Requirements Map

Issue #15 asks for structured peer reviews, review modes, reviewer history, transparent reputation metrics, badges, leaderboards, and incentive tiers.

This module covers the fairness boundary before review events affect those surfaces:

- Structured peer review: validates review-event packets before reputation updates.
- Review modes: protects anonymous and double-blind reviews from accommodation disclosure leaks.
- Reviewer profiles and histories: freezes late/decline penalties when platform accommodations were not honored.
- Reputation scoring: prevents negative deltas from accessibility or locale process failures.
- Badges and leaderboards: holds unsafe service-credit changes before downstream publication.
- Transparent incentives: emits reviewer-safe findings, required actions, and stable audit digests.

## Non-Overlap

This is not another broad reputation ledger, endorsement ring guard, badge renewal gate, leaderboard eligibility guard, civility/moderation checker, review timeliness module, recusal/COI guard, calibration bench, edit-history integrity guard, identity-leak-only guard, profile visibility guard, CRediT role evidence guard, reviewer workload guard, reviewer expertise credential guard, or peer-review template rubric guard.

The slice focuses specifically on accessibility and accommodation fairness before reputation penalties or service-credit deltas are applied.
