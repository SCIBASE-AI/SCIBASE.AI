Bounty Intake Compliance Gate Demo

1. Start the local API:
   PORT=4364 npm start

2. Inspect the dashboard:
   curl -fsS http://localhost:4364/api/dashboard

3. Validate a publish-ready biotech challenge:
   curl -fsS 'http://localhost:4364/api/validate?id=challenge-biomarker-rna'

4. Inspect a held public climate challenge:
   curl -fsS 'http://localhost:4364/api/publish-decision?id=challenge-climate-forecast'

5. Score a qualified participant application:
   curl -fsS 'http://localhost:4364/api/prequalification?challenge=challenge-biomarker-rna&application=app-immune-lab'

6. Export sponsor audit evidence:
   curl -fsS 'http://localhost:4364/api/audit-packet?id=challenge-biomarker-rna'

Expected review highlights:
- The biomarker challenge is publish-ready: verified sponsor, funded escrow, complete rubric, NDA, prequalification, and IP protections.
- The climate challenge is held because it has missing scientific context, an incomplete rubric total, and unfunded escrow.
- One participant team qualifies and one is blocked until ORCID, institution, conflict disclosure, and NDA are complete.
- Audit packets include deterministic evidence hashes for sponsor review.
