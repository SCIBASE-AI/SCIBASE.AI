## Non-Overlap Note

This submission is distinct from broad knowledge graph extractors, graph navigators, link audits, knowledge-gap explorers, ontology drift migration, relationship conflict arbitration, author-affiliation disambiguation, artifact reuse lineage, evidence freshness checks, instrument-method compatibility graphs, and reproducibility route planners. It focuses specifically on visibility, embargo, consent, license, and clearance gating before graph recommendations are displayed.
