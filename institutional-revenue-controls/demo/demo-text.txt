Institutional Revenue Controls Demo

1. Start the local API:
   PORT=4363 npm start

2. Validate the finance data:
   curl -fsS http://localhost:4363/api/validate

3. Inspect account controls:
   curl -fsS http://localhost:4363/api/dashboard

4. Reconcile the draft invoice:
   curl -fsS 'http://localhost:4363/api/invoice?id=inv-2026-05-draft'

5. Inspect approval routing:
   curl -fsS 'http://localhost:4363/api/approval-route?id=inv-2026-05-draft'

6. Export audit evidence:
   curl -fsS http://localhost:4363/api/audit-export

Expected review highlights:
- The account is valid and configured as an institutional enterprise customer.
- Usage events are priced deterministically.
- The draft invoice matches usage lines but is held because one grant budget crosses its hard limit.
- Approval routing shows pending finance approval.
- Audit export includes deterministic evidence hashes for finance review.
